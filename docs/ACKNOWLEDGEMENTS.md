## Target threshold

The target threshold follows the [CDC A1C guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/diabetes-testing/prediabetes-a1c-test.html): normal below 5.7%, prediabetes 5.7–6.4%, and diabetes at least 6.5%.
